Bowen Bao

I'm a software engineer focused on health and science data infrastructure. I care about systems that make consequential things legible — whether that's a patient's trajectory through a hospital, a genome sequence, or the movement of light across water. Outside of engineering I practice ikebana and watercolor, both of which are, at bottom, about the same problem: how do you represent something real without losing what made it worth representing.
